# Research corrections to apply (open)

Found by research agents; not yet applied to code or data. Verify the sources before changing anything.

- **Stångehuvud:** src/lighthouses.js says dark "since the 1990s"; three sources (island-lore research,
  2026-11-07) say the light went out in **1940**. Matters for the rewind and the 1950 world.
- **Islandsberg's old lantern:** LIGHTHOUSES.md puts it on the ridge; one source says it was in an attic
  room in the gable. Check before modelling it.
- **Lysekil around 1703:** not a hamlet: a fishing place and church from the 1500s, the pilot station from
  the early 1700s, town charter 1903. Check docs/PEOPLE-1700.md, data/people-1700.json and Torhild's file.
- **Gullholmen's harbour wreck:** felled no earlier than the 1720s; the museum rejects the Tordenskiold
  legend; one treenail is fire-damaged (the ship may have burned).
