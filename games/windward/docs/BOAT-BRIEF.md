# Boat brief (Felix, 2026-11-05)

Decided in an interactive session with Felix. Build in the existing style: low-poly, flat
Lambert, Skagen palette (shared/ART.md). Keep src/boat.js's API: createBoatMesh(color) →
{ group, setSailAngle } with the boom swinging to leeward, so phone controls keep working.

## Today's coast (the lure): "Svalan", granddad's old koster
- A traditional **kosterbåt**: a double-ended wooden sailing boat (pointed at both ends),
  inherited from granddad. Roughly 7–9 m real; it must still read clearly at the game camera.
- **Hull: varnished wood**, warm honey planks with visible clinker/plank lines; a cared-for
  old boat.
- **Sails: classic white** (modern start). Traditional rig for a koster: gaff or sprit
  main plus a jib; pick what reads best from above and still swings as one boom.
- **Name "Svalan"** ("the Swallow"): the boat's name in the story only. Felix removed the name
  board from the model (2026-11-05: "looks off").

## After the jump to ~1700: a small eka with a spritsail
- A small **clinker eka** (open wooden boat), ~4–5 m real, one **spritsail**. Poor and
  humble: you start with almost nothing.
- **Hull: dark tarred wood.** **Sails: tanned brown/red** (barked sails).
- **Oars** lie in the boat (used in calm or in harbour).

## Both boats
- **Friends are told apart by a pennant at the masthead** in each player's colour (the
  sails are no longer the player colour).
- **Things on deck:** the deck slots are visible (a fish crate, a barrel, granddad's chest
  later).
- **Lantern at night:** a small warm lantern that lights at dusk (ties to the day-night cycle).
- **Worn, mended look:** patches and a repaired plank; the boat shows its history and can be
  fixed up later (upgrade hook; ties to Brita's boatbuilder father).
