# Unique artifact candidates: Felix picks

Fourteen candidates for the "unique" tier in `docs/ALIVE-SEA.md` (uniques are only ever placed by Felix). Each is
**inspired by a real fact** (source given) but the object itself is INVENTED: the history is real, the thing is ours.
Written 2026-11-07. Source ids resolve in `data/island-lore.json` (`sources`). Rarity guess is for the tier below
unique if Felix prefers not to make it unique.

Format: **name** · place · made / found · source · fits the story because · leads to · caveat.

## Chapter 1 and the canon

1. **Brass lantern plate stamped 1954** · Kråksundsgap · made 1954, found 1950 · canon + RAÄ sea marks (Kråksundsgap
   leading marks 1926/1941) · A plate from a tower that does not exist yet: the proof that the glimpse of the 1954
   light was real. Fits the "light from the future" and the finale (the restored 1941 lantern in Lysekil's
   roundabout) · leads to: the finale easter egg, a quest to return it to 1954 or to Lysekil · caveat: pure canon
   extension, no source for any plate.
2. **The keeper's second log** · Måseskär · made 1950, found 1950 · repo light data (Måseskär manned 1865-1997,
   staff of three) · The keeper writes down lights that should not be there; the book is how the first sign that
   time is strange becomes an object · leads to: Torhild's era (the same bearings in 1703) · caveat: invented book;
   no keeper is named anywhere (rule: no named 1950 keeper).
3. **Måseskär's brass clockwork pinion** · Måseskär · made 1865, found 1950 / today · sv.wikipedia Måseskär (brass
   clockwork and weights preserved; electric motor 1955) · A wheel from the machine that turned the light: the
   keepers wound it. Rare in 1950 (still running), unique if you carry it to today's foundation · leads to the
   2015 relight story and the old tower today · caveat: whether a gear was ever lost is invented.

## Käringön and Gullholmen

4. **The anchor nobody claims** · Käringön harbour · made 1700s?, found 1703 / today · RAÄ L1960:7197 (an iron stock
   anchor raised 1995 or 1996 from the harbour roads, moved ashore) · An anchor that has outlasted its ship; you can
   put it back in the water in one era and raise it in another · leads to: a "whose anchor?" quest across eras ·
   caveat: nobody knows whose it was: keep it that way.
5. **Treenail from the old keel** · Käringön · made 1516-1648, found 1703 / 1950 / today · RAÄ L1960:5601 (a 9 m
   keel timber, stem knee and plank; tree-rings date the wood to 1516-1648) · A peg from a ship already a ruin in
   1703. A real, dated find: easy to tell as "real history" in the logbook · leads to: the boys who dive for the
   "Spanish ship" in 1703 · caveat: the ship's nationality is unknown.
6. **Strömstierna's sea-chest key** · Käringön · made 1690s, found 1703 · sv.wikipedia Olof Strömstierna;
   history-start-area.md (born on Käringön 1664, East Indies to 1697, admiralty captain 1700) · A historical public
   figure's key: the island's pride, the link to the Great Northern War (and, by our inference, Dynekilen 1716) ·
   leads to: a mother's letter to her sea-officer son; a Torhild quest · caveat: he is a historical public figure with
   sources; whether he commanded at Dynekilen is our inference (low).
7. **The Tordenskjold plank** · Gullholmen harbour · made 1727-1827 (tree-rings), found 1950 · Bohusläns museum rapport
   2012:39 via history-start-area.md (harbour wreck found 1936; the legend that it is Tordenskjold's is rejected by
   the museum) · A plank the old men swear is Tordenskjold's. Felix decides what it means: a fraud, a joke, or the
   real thing in the story · leads to: the Saturday argument on the quay · caveat: the museum's dating makes the
   legend false; keep that visible as "real history".

## Grundsund and Lysekil

8. **The first net-cast purse** · Grundsund · made 1798-1799, found 1950 · sv.wikipedia Grundsund (the chapel's money
   came from one herring cast whose profit was given; first service 5 October 1799) · A small leather purse of the
   coins from that cast. A village's founding act as an object · leads to: the church bell and the founding story ·
   caveat: no source for any purse.
9. **The chapel's ship's bell** · Grundsund · made ?, found 1950 · sv.wikipedia Grundsund (a ship's bell was used
   until the tower and bell of 1818) · A bell that was once on a ship and then a church: the village's sea and its faith
   in one thing · leads to: the herring-cast purse; a tow quest · caveat: where the bell went is not known.
10. **A ball bearing wrapped in oilcloth** · Lysekil · made 1943, found 1950 · sv.wikipedia Lysekil (Operation Bridford:
    gunboats ran ball bearings from Lysekil to England through the Skagerrak blockade, autumn 1943 to winter 1944; resumed
    1944-45 with supplies for the Danish resistance) · A tiny object from a secret war that left the harbour quiet by
    day. For a 1950 child it is a puzzle: why would anyone hide a bearing · leads to: the depot clerk's memory, a
    "night boat" rumour · caveat: nothing says one was lost.
11. **Wilhelmson's harbour sketch** · Fiskebäckskil · made c.1900-1928, found 1950 · sv.wikipedia Fiskebäckskil (the
    painter Carl Wilhelmson, born there 1866, a mate's son; villa and studio above the village) · A small oil sketch of
    the harbour as it looked then: shows Britta's home from above, a view that includes things since gone · leads to:
    the Kaptensgatan walk, the opening house in the opening scene · caveat: a public historical figure; the sketch and
    its attribution are invented, mark it so; do not claim it is a real painting.

## Skaftö and the deep past

12. **Clinker plank with an iron rivet from the Gåsöfjorden wreck** · Gåsö / Skaftö coast · made medieval, found
    1950 / today · RAÄ 270, "the Skaftö wreck", a medieval ship in Gåsöfjorden excavated 2006-2009 by Bohusläns museum
    (seen in a search summary of a Digitaltmuseum record; the record itself not opened) · The oldest thing you can find
    in the window: an object from before any sea mark · leads to: Vrakevik's story (the bay's name is "wreck bay";
    no source ties the wreck to that bay) · caveat: do not tie it to Vrakevik without a source.
13. **Iron ring from the Gäven pole** · Gäven · made 1835, found 1950 · RAÄ via Söderlund 2007 (a 15 m red pole mark with
    a pilots' lookout platform among the stays; replaced by a light in 1917) · An iron footing from a pilot lookout:
    Torhild's descendants kept watch there · leads to: Britta's father's "out at the old pole", pilot quests · caveat:
    the ring is our invention; no source says one survives.
14. **The Anna Maria's chain link** · Bonden, west of Grundsund · made c.1870s?, found 1950 · RAÄ L1960:6098 (the
    schooner Anna Maria, 10 February 1905, in a north-west gale: the chain parted, she drifted onto Bonden's west side;
    3 died) · A rare in 1950 (the wreck is still in living memory); unique if linked to a family you meet in 1703 or a
    quest about names on a headstone · leads to: Bonden's cairn dare; a respectful memorial quest · caveat: real deaths:
    handle with care and no named people.
