## Story
**(lore)** Brita is a **boatbuilder's daughter** from the Grundsund area: an ordinary working
family, not posh. She knows boats inside out, can fix yours and explain why it sails the way
it does, and knows the hidden coves and sounds. She lives with her **grandmother**, who tells
the old stories.

**(lore)** She is the one who **pulls you ashore** after the storm throws you back to ~1700.

## Role in play
**(lore)** She **teaches you to sail and live in this era** by setting **little tasks**
("meet me at the cairn by the sound"), each teaching one thing: trim, tacking, reading sea
marks, mooring, the first claim and build.

## What she wants
**(lore)** To **see beyond the fog**. She has glimpsed the flashing fog before and never dared
go in. You are proof that it leads somewhere.

## Her secret (learned later)
**(lore)** **She has seen you before**, in a dream or in the fog. Mysterious; it pays off much
later (a cross-era echo).

## How she decides
**(lore)** Free and daring: bends rules, takes risks, loves the open sea.
