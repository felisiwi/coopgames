**(lore)** Brita is a pilot's daughter from the Grundsund area, but nothing like a fancy
pilot's girl: barefoot, salt-haired, at home in every hidden cove and sound. Her father is

**(lore)** She is the one who **pulls you ashore** after the storm throws you back to ~1700.

go in. You are proof that it leads somewhere.
