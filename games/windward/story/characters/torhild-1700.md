## Story
**(lore)** Torhild is a **pilot's daughter** from the Grundsund area: a working family, not posh.
Her father guides ships through the skerries (Sweden's state pilot service was founded by decree
in 1696, and compulsory pilotage dates from 1667; see docs/WORLD-1700.md). With no lighthouses
yet, the pilots' knowledge of cairns, marks and leading lines is how the coast is read, and
she has it. She lives with her **grandmother**, who tells the old stories.
**(name)** Torhild / Torild: Þórr (thunder) + hildr (battle), "thunder fight". It fits the storm
that brings you to her.

**(lore)** She is the one who **pulls you ashore** after the storm throws you back to ~1700.

## Role in play
**(lore)** She **teaches you to sail and live in this era** by setting **little tasks**
("meet me at the cairn by the sound"), each teaching one thing: trim, tacking, reading sea
marks, mooring, the first claim and build.

## What she wants
**(lore)** To **see beyond the fog**. She has glimpsed the flashing fog before and never dared
go in. You are proof that it leads somewhere.

## Her secret (learned later)
**(lore)** **She has seen you before**, in a dream or in the fog. Mysterious; it pays off much
later (a cross-era echo).

## How she decides
**(lore)** Free and daring: bends rules (even her father's pilot rules), takes risks, loves the open sea.
