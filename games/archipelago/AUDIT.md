## 2026-11-04 — first audit

Audited at commit c81ce3a. Summary: 1 pass · 3 weak · 2 fail · 3 needs playtest.

### Hub-wide
| # | Check | Rating | Evidence | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | First 30 seconds | needs playtest | No in-game prompt at all: the canvas shows only the island and a dot; controls (WASD/arrows via `shared/input.js`) are never shown (`game.js`, `src/render.js` draw no text in normal play). Walking is discoverable by pressing keys, but *why* you'd walk isn't stated anywhere. | Watch for: does each player start moving within ~10 s, and do they know they're looking for each other without being told? If not, add one fading line on spawn: "Your friend is somewhere on this island. Find them." |
| 2 | Both players matter | weak | The other player only affects you once they're inside your vision radius (`game.js:138-143` draws them only when `fog === 2`); otherwise solo and two-player play are identical. The only shared goal — finding each other — is implicit. | Give the reunion a payoff: e.g. once both players have seen each other, fog lifts around both of them together (shared reveal), so being together explores faster than apart. |
| 3 | A "come here" moment | needs playtest | The landmark is the only thing on the island worth pointing at (one tile per seed, `src/island.js:235-237`), and there's no way to signal "I'm here" or "look at this" in-game (the only message is `{t:'pos'}`, `game.js:97-106`). Voice happens outside the game, so it can still work. | Watch for: does either player say "come look at this" — about anything? If the only call-out is "where are you?", add more discoverables (see per-game check 1). |
| 4 | Graceful end | fail | The game has no stopping point — no reunion moment, no "island explored", nothing that says "that was a session". Drops aren't handled either: the hub's `net.onClose(showLost)` (`index.html:194, 261`) only switches a panel inside `#lobby`, and `#lobby` is hidden during play (`index.html:140`), so "Connection lost" never appears mid-game. The game never reads message age, so a departed partner's dot stays frozen where they left (`game.js:97-101`). | Make the reunion an event: when the players first see each other, show a small shared moment ("Found you!") — a natural place to stop or carry on. Separately (hub-wide fix, see below): surface the lost-connection message over the game canvas. |
| 5 | Hub contract | pass | `game.json` `id` matches the folder; `game.js:23` default-exports `start({ canvas, net, seed, role, players })`; world comes only from `generateIsland(seed)` (same field from the same seed, `src/serendipity.test.js` "same seed" test); no PeerJS/`shared/net.js` import (`game.js:2-3`); assets resolve via `import.meta.url` (`src/render.js:12`). | — |
| 6 | True to its own feel | weak | AGENTS.md sets "cozy and beautiful, not programmer-art". Terrain uses a curated CC0 tile set, but both players are plain filled circles (`src/render.js:143`), and the HUD is the dev page's monospace seed label. | Replace the circles with two small character sprites from the same tile family (or a matching CC0 pack), host and guest in different colours. |

### Per-game (design-checks.md)
| Check | Rating | Evidence | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| Every stretch of island has something worth finding | fail | Dead-zone % (walkable tiles > 10 walking steps from anything discoverable, `src/serendipity.js`): **seed 1: 79.8%** (873/1094) · **seed 2: 78.2%** (794/1015) · **seed 3: 80.0%** (884/1105). Target is under 25%. The only discoverable is one `landmark` tile per island; farthest tile 28–35 steps from it. See it with `?debug=serendipity`. | Add a scatter of small discoverables (a cairn, a boathouse, a lost oar, a lone birch…) placed from the seed with a minimum spacing of ~2×`SERENDIPITY_DEAD_ZONE_TILES` apart, re-measure until under 25%. |
| Finding each other takes a few minutes, not zero and not forever | needs playtest | Walking distance between spawns: **28 / 24 / 25 steps** (seeds 1–3), against `VISION_RADIUS` 5 — players start well out of sight, as intended (`CONFIG.SPAWN_DISTANCE` 18 Manhattan). At `MOVE_SECONDS_PER_TILE` 0.18 that's ~5 s of walking if you knew where to go; the minutes come entirely from not knowing. Nothing points players toward each other. | Watch for: time from spawn to first sighting. Under ~30 s is too easy; over ~3 min with no hint is frustrating. If too long, add a faint directional cue (e.g. footprints, or the other player's trail showing in seen fog). |
| The island reads as a place, not a noise field | weak | Land tile mix is near-identical across seeds — sand 11%, grass 19–21%, rock 21–27%, forest 41–47%, one landmark. Rock and forest are noise-patched (`src/island.js:161-170`), so there are regions, but no named features (no bay, peak, clearing, path) to describe to the other player. Visual judgement → Felix via `?spot=1..3`. | Give each island one or two named features from the seed (a meadow clearing, a rocky summit around the landmark, a sandy cove) so players have something to say "I'm by the ___". |

### Most important next step
Add things to find. The 78–80% dead zone is the root of three other findings: it's why there's no "come here" moment beyond "where are you?", why the island doesn't read as a place, and why solo and co-op feel the same. A seeded scatter of a handful of small discoverables, spaced so the dead-zone measurement drops under 25%, would move checks 2, 3 and the island-as-place check at once — and the serendipity overlay now shows exactly where they're needed.
